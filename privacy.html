Privacy policy

Gruppetto Director · last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

Gruppetto Director collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your career is saved only on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your careers, settings and backups are stored on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. A backup file you export goes only where you choose to send it.

Things that use the internet, only when you tap them

  • Submit a bug opens your mail app with a draft that includes the game's version, your phone model, iOS version and screen size. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to fix the bug, and we don't share it.
  • Load a names file from a link downloads the file from the address you give. No information about you is sent with the request beyond what any web download sends (such as your IP address, seen by the site hosting the file).
  • Follow the creator links open Strava, YouTube or Instagram in your browser or their app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: gruppetto.manager@icloud.com.
