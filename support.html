Support

Gruppetto Director, a pro cycling team manager

Found a bug or have an idea?

In the game, go to More → App and updates → Submit a bug. It opens an email with your version and device filled in. Or write to gruppetto.manager@icloud.com. A screenshot or a backup file helps a lot.

Moving or keeping a career

Your career is saved on your phone. To keep a copy or move it to another phone, use More → Backup to export a file, then restore it under More → Backup on the other phone.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
